Privacy Policy

Last updated 15 October 2026

1. Summary

Stellina lets your child fall asleep to a voice they trust: yours. That makes your voice and your child's details the most sensitive things we hold, and we treat them that way. We collect only what the App needs, we store it in the EU where we can, we never sell it, we never use it for advertising, and you can delete everything from the App at any time.

2. Who is responsible

Stellina AS, organisation number 938 111 529, Lindemans gate 3C, 0267 Oslo, Norway, is the data controller. Questions about privacy: privacy@stellina.app.

4. About your child

Stellina is used by parents and caregivers, not by children. Your child does not have an account, and we only process the details you enter. Do not enter more about your child than the App asks for.

5. How your voice is processed

First, you read a short text aloud in the App. The recording is sent, encrypted, to our server and from there to ElevenLabs, our voice technology provider, which creates a voice model. As soon as the audio has been made, the voice model is deleted at ElevenLabs. Your recording is kept encrypted in our own storage in the EU, so new audio can be made later without you recording again. The App asks for each story once and stores the finished audio in your household's private storage and on your phone. Playback never sends your voice anywhere again. ElevenLabs processes your voice only on our instructions, under a data processing agreement, and may not use it to train its models.

You can withdraw consent, record again or delete your voice at any time under Privacy & Security. We then delete your recording, any voice model at ElevenLabs and the audio made with it.

We may disclose data if the law requires it, for example to the police with a valid order. If Stellina AS is sold or merged, your data may be transferred to the new owner, who must follow this policy. We will tell you in advance.

7. Security

Data is encrypted in transit and at rest. Each household can only see its own data, enforced in the database. Child photos and audio are kept in private storage. Only a small number of people at Stellina have technical access, and nobody at Stellina listens to your recording or your child's audio.

8. Your rights

You can ask for access to your data, correction, deletion, restriction, a copy to take elsewhere (data portability), and you can object to processing based on legitimate interest. Where we rely on consent, you can withdraw it at any time without affecting what was done before.

In the App you can export your data and delete your account under Account and Privacy & Security. Deleting your account deletes your account, your voice recording and any voice model, your child's profile, photo and audio, and your history. If you are the last member of the household, the whole household is deleted. Copies in our backups are removed within 30 days.

For anything else, write to privacy@stellina.app. We answer within one month. You can also complain to the Norwegian Data Protection Authority (Datatilsynet) or the authority where you live.

9. Changes

If we change this policy in a way that matters to you, we will tell you in the App before the change applies. If a change requires new consent, we will ask for it.
