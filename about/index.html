Our story

Built around the voice they trust most.

Personalised sleep and comfort for your child, shaped by your voice. Made in Oslo, coming soon to iPhone.

Every family has its own way of saying goodnight. A few familiar words, a song, a voice in the dark that says you are safe. Stellina began on one of those evenings, with a simple question: what if that voice could be there every time it was needed?

Stellina’s story

When Elliot was three months old, something shifted. Falling asleep suddenly needed more. More sound, more presence, more comfort.

I tried everything. White noise. Playlists. Different apps.

Some of it worked, but never on its own.

Every nap, three times a day, and again in the evening, I found myself repeating the same ritual. Switching between apps, starting the music, then humming and singing on top of it to help him settle.

That is when it became clear.

The thing that actually worked was not the sound.
It was the voice.

Not an app. Not a playlist. Something familiar. Something he already knew.

So I started to wonder. What if that part did not have to depend on me being there every single time? What if it could be simple?

One tap, and your child hears you. Familiar. Predictable. Safe.

That is where Stellina began.

Kristina Krøvel, founder

What we believe

Your voice stays yours.

Your recording is used only for your family’s stories. It is never sold, never used for advertising and never used to train AI models. You can delete it at any time.

Calm by design.

No ads and no overstimulation. Stellina is made for parents, not as a screen for your child, and it steps back once the music begins.

Made with care.

All of Stellina’s music is original and composed for sleep, carried by one recognisable Stellina theme on felt piano and music box.

Why we call it Stellina

In Italian, stellina means little star. It is also what parents call a small child they love. Buonanotte, stellina. Goodnight, little star.

A little star is one of the first lights to appear when the evening sky turns dark. It does not chase the night away. It simply stays there, small and steady, until morning.

That is what we want Stellina to be for your child.
A familiar light in the dark, in the voice they know best.

The star is in our icon, in every universe and in the Stellina theme that opens each evening. Stellina is made by Stellina AS in Oslo, Norway, where winter evenings come early and the stars are out long before bedtime.
