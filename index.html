Long before they know your face, they know your voice. Stellina is built on that bond – and on the oldest sleep advice there is: the same calm ritual, every night.

Recognised from birth

Within days of birth, newborns recognise their mother’s voice and prefer it to a stranger’s (DeCasper & Fifer, Science, 1980).

Routine is the foundation

Health services recommend a calm, predictable bedtime routine – dim lights, quiet voices, a lullaby or story (NHS, Helsenorge).

Questions

Good to know before tonight.

My child won’t sleep. Can Stellina help?

Stellina gives you a calm, consistent bedtime routine in your own voice, the kind of routine health services recommend. It is not a medical treatment. If you are worried about your child’s sleep, talk to your health visitor or doctor.

What is Stellina?

Stellina is a sleep app for children, made for parents. It combines original sleep music with your own voice, recorded once and turned into calm Bedtime, Nap and Comfort stories that say your child’s name.

How does Stellina use my voice?

You read a short text aloud, in less than a minute. Stellina uses AI to create the stories in your voice, with your child’s name, laid over the music. Your voice is used only for your family’s stories. It is never sold, never used for advertising and never used to train AI models.

What is the difference between Bedtime, Nap and Comfort?

Bedtime is for the evening, Nap for daytime rest and Comfort for any moment your child needs you close. Each universe opens with your voice and your child’s name, followed by its own original music of about 15 minutes. The music loops seamlessly, so it plays for as long as your child needs.

How much does Stellina cost?

The first seven nights are free. After that Stellina Premium is €79.99 a year or €14.99 a month, and the App Store shows the price in your currency. On the yearly plan that is about €0.22 a night. Used three times a day, for a nap, at bedtime and when your child wakes in the night, it comes to about 7 cents each time. Without a subscription you can still play the short First evening preview.

Which devices does Stellina work on?

Stellina is for iPhone with iOS 18 or later. Versions for other phones are on the way.

Is my child’s data safe?

Your recording is encrypted and used only to create the voice in your child’s universe. Data is stored in the EU and handled under GDPR by Stellina AS in Oslo, Norway.
