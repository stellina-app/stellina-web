Beta test

Help us test Stellina.

Stellina is a sleep app for children. You record your voice once, and Stellina uses it to say calm, reassuring words over gentle sleep music, so your child feels safe and settles more easily. Before it reaches the App Store, we would love your honest eyes on it.

  • About 2 minutes to start
  • Everything is free, Premium included
  • Open until Sunday 29 November

Get started

  1. Step 1

    Download TestFlight

    TestFlight is Apple’s own app for testing new apps before they launch.

    Get TestFlight
  2. Step 2

    Open the invitation on your iPhone

    Tap the button below on the iPhone you want to test on.

    Join the Stellina beta
  3. Step 3

    Install and open Stellina

    Follow the steps in the app. Recording your voice takes about a minute.

  4. Step 4

    Add the Stellina widget

    Start Stellina with one tap, straight from your phone.

    • Lock Screen: touch and hold the Lock Screen, tap Customise, choose the Lock Screen, tap Add Widgets and choose Stellina.
    • Home Screen: touch and hold an empty area, tap Edit, then Add Widget, search for Stellina and choose a widget.

Three small things we hope you’ll do

  1. 1

    Write down your first impression

    Right after you open the app for the first time. What did you think and feel? A short note or message is perfect.

  2. 2

    Use it for a few days

    Use Bedtime in the evening for a few nights, and try Nap during the day and Comfort when your child wakes or needs you close.

  3. 3

    Fill in the short form

    When you have used Stellina a little. It takes about five minutes.

    Open the form
Spotted something odd? Take a screenshot in the app and tap Share Beta Feedback. It comes straight to us.
About your voice. Your recording is used only to create the audio for your child, and Stellina does not keep it. You can delete your voice and everything in your account at any time in the app.

If you don’t have time for everything, that is completely fine. A little is much better than nothing, and honest criticism helps us more than praise.

Thank you for helping.
